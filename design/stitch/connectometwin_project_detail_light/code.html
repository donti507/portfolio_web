01 / Motivation

The Question

Static connectome schematics and flat 2D network graphs fundamentally obscure how ring attractors navigate three-dimensional biological geometries. In the Drosophila central complex, compass navigation relies on a toroidal manifold mapped onto the Ellipsoid Body (EB), whose columnar projections route through the Protocerebral Bridge (PB) and the Noduli (NO).

When neural computational models assume idealized all-to-all or symmetric cosine-tuned connectivity, they gloss over anatomical asymmetry and individual synaptic variation. How does this ring attractor sustain stable representation when confronted with real, reconstructed EM synaptic distributions? Can an interactive, client-side tool allow immediate visual hypothesis testing of circuit perturbations without requiring multi-gigabyte neuroglancer clusters?

02 / Scientific Impact

Why It Matters

Until recently, interrogating whole-brain connectomic datasets like the Janelia Hemibrain required specialized python tooling, local cloud clusters, or heavy Neuroglancer instances with substantial bandwidth overhead.

ConnectomeTwin democratizes structural inspection for both experimental neurophysiologists and machine intelligence researchers. By distilling dense skeleton reconstructions into optimized instanced geometry, researchers can alter individual synaptic matrices, inject simulated current into wedge neurons, and watch the attractor bump drift or dissolve in real time directly within an educational or laboratory setting.

03 / Engineering Specification

How It's Built

A purpose-engineered spatial pipeline combining modern client-side graphics standards with rigorous neuroinformatics validation.

layers

1. Rendering Architecture

Utilizes Three.js paired with custom GLSL instancing shaders. Skeletons extracted from Janelia NeuPrint are pre-processed into lightweight hierarchical Level-of-Detail (LOD) payloads:

  • Overview LOD: 2.6 MB compressed quantized buffer containing midline skeletal spline centroids.
  • Full-Detail Morphology: 10.2 MB uncompressed exact arborization mesh with calibrated micro-radii.
  • Shader Pipeline: Single-pass screen-space ambient occlusion (SSAO) and depth-aware glowing line segments.
verified

05 / Scope & Limitations

What This Does Not Show

info
Simplified Synaptic Morphology

Individual synapse locations are rendered as cylindrical spline anchor points rather than full EM volumetric reconstructions (t-bars and PSD meshes). This design choice preserves client-side interactivity across mobile browsers.

query_stats
Pre-computed ODE Integration vs Live Biophysics

Attractor trajectories reflect numerical solutions to linearized rate-based differential equations pre-computed across a parameter grid. Live compartment biophysical simulations (e.g. Hodgkin-Huxley NEURON models) are reserved for companion offline cluster runs.

Section 01

The Question

In competitive diving, judging is subjective, prone to national biases, and vulnerable to viewing angles. But human flight after the diver loses contact with the platform follows strict Newtonian mechanics. If standard broadcast television cameras can record keypoints accurately, could an automated physics engine evaluate dive consistency without manual calibration grids?

Invariant Conservation Bounds Eq. (1.1 – 1.2)
y(t) = y₀ + v₀t − ½gt²
dL / dt = 0 &implies ∑ I_i(t) · ω_i(t) = L₀

Where I_i(t) is the moment of inertia tensor of each anatomical limb segment, and ω_i(t) is instantaneous segment angular velocity derived from consecutive keypoint coordinates. For angular momentum L to be conserved throughout flight, instantaneous derivatives must sum to zero within observation error bounds.

Section 02

Why It Matters

Standard benchmarks such as MS-COCO, Human3.6M, and MPII report average precision (mAP) over predominantly upright, static, or pedestrian walking postures. These benchmarks create an illusion of mastery: modern models frequently score upwards of 85 mAP.

Yet, when deployed on non-canonical human configurations—such as an inverted 3.5 somersault with tuck at 18 rad/s—standard architectural priors shatter. The camera shutter smears limb edges, limbs cross the coronal plane in fractions of a second, and joint coordinates fluctuate across frames. Even a median error of 9.5 pixels introduces sufficient noise into numerical differentiation that estimated torque fluctuates wildly, masking any true physiological variation.

Section 03

What I Did

01

Dataset Curation & Dual Annotation

Extracted 24,000 synchronized frames across 120 platform dives from World Aquatics TV feeds. Double-annotated 1,200 anchor frames across three human researchers to construct a gold-standard ground-truth baseline with known annotator variance.

02

Kinematic Curve Fitting

Calculated continuous parabolic trajectories for the body center of mass (CoM). Deviations from freefall ballistic trajectories (−½gt²) established a lower bound for spatial measurement drift.

03

Acrobatics Domain Fine-Tuning

Fine-tuned ViTPose-H, RTMPose-l, and NSAQA architectures using rotational augmentation, inverted synthetic postures, and temporal smoothing losses designed specifically for high angular acceleration.

04

Physics-Residual Quantification

Propagated keypoint coordinate series through Hanavan's human segment body model. Quantified unphysical angular momentum injection across phases as a function of keypoint uncertainty.

Section 04

What I Found

Fine-tuning substantially cleans up catastrophic limb-swapping and temporal jumps, but leaves the underlying precision ceiling unbroken.

Section 05

What This Does Not Show

balance Deliberate Boundary Conditions

This monograph does not claim that automatic dive adjudication is theoretically impossible. Rather, it reveals the limits of single-camera broadcast-grade 2D pose networks without physical priors. Specifically, this evaluation:

  • Omits Water Entry: Analysis terminates 100ms before water impact. Splashes and hydrodynamic occlusion are excluded from this error floor.
  • Monocular Perspective: Broadcast lenses suffer from non-linear radial zoom and perspective foreshortening that standard intrinsic camera matrices approximate poorly.
  • Zero Dynamic Priors: Evaluated models process frames sequentially without bidirectional kinematic smoothing filters (e.g., Rauch-Tung-Striebel smoothers).
Section 06

Reproduce It

The full evaluation harness, including pretrained checkpoints, Hanavan segment inertia weights, and CLI residual metrics calculator, is open source.

bash — residual_eval.py
# 1. Clone repository and install dependencies
git clone https://github.com/aliashrafyad/physics-residual-diving.git
cd physics-residual-diving && pip install -r requirements.txt

# 2. Run residual evaluator against reference dive sequence
python evaluate.py \
  --model nsaqa-acrobatics \
  --video samples/10m_platform_042.mp4 \
  --bound-precision-px 3.0 \
  --export-svg-gauge
Section 07

What's Next

The conclusion is definite: computer vision alone cannot resolve keypoint jitter to the degree physical laws demand. In my upcoming work, I am formulating this as a Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) where Newton's equations of rotational motion act as an explicit hard constraint on the loss function, rather than an afterthought evaluator.

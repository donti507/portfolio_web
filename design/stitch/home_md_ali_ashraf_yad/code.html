Md Ali Ashraf Yad · Wrocław

I study how much our models of the brain depend on the data we build them from.

I'm a third-year Applied Computer Science student at Wrocław University of Science and Technology. My work asks when connectome-based models can be trusted, how to run large ML experiments honestly on HPC clusters, and how to check LLM outputs before people rely on them. I preregister what I can and report the results that fail.
